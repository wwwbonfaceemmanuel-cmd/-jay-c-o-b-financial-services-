Our method

The same four steps, whatever the brief.

Structure is what makes advice repeatable. Every mandate follows the sequence below, with a written deliverable at each stage.

48hTypical response time to a new enquiry
10 daysAverage time to a signed-off plan

Understand

A structured discovery session covering income, obligations, goals and constraints — plus the documents behind them.

Diagnose

We model the current position, stress-test it, and identify the two or three levers that will actually change the outcome.

Recommend

A written plan with options, costs and trade-offs. You approve the route before any application or product is touched.

Deliver

We execute alongside you — drafting, chasing, negotiating — then review progress on a fixed calendar.

Questions

Before you book

A few things clients usually ask on the first call.

How quickly can we start?

Discovery calls are usually available within three working days. Most engagements begin within a week of that call, once the scope and fee are confirmed in writing.

Do you charge for the first meeting?

No. The first 30-minute discovery call is free. If we are not the right fit, we will tell you there and point you elsewhere where we can.

Are you tied to a particular bank or insurer?

No. We work with a range of licensed institutions and disclose any referral arrangement before you instruct us. We are not paid by lenders to place business.

Can you help if I am outside Lilongwe?

Yes. Roughly half of our clients are outside Lilongwe and work with us by phone, WhatsApp and video. We also run monthly clinics in Kasungu, Salima and Mulanje.

What if I have already been declined?

That is one of the most common starting points. We review the decision, identify what drove it, and build a realistic remediation plan rather than simply resubmitting.

Do you offer services in Chichewa?

Yes. Sessions can be conducted in English or Chichewa, and we can arrange Yao or Tumbuka interpretation for group workshops with notice.
