Eligibility

Before you apply

Most Malawi residents over 18 can qualify.

We assess affordability, not just a credit score. If you can show stable income and a clear plan for repayment, there is usually a product that fits.

  • Aged 18 or older with a valid national ID or passport
  • Resident in Malawi with a verifiable address
  • Stable income from employment, business or farming
  • Monthly repayment comfortably within your disposable income
  • Business applicants: 6+ months of operation and records

Questions

FAQ

Answers before you apply.

Still unsure? Our help centre covers applications, repayments and account security in more detail.

Visit help centre Call +265 999 123 456
How much can I borrow?

From MWK 50,000 to MWK 80,000,000 depending on the product. Personal loans run to MWK 15 million, business loans to MWK 50 million and secured loans to MWK 80 million. The approved amount is based on affordability.

How long does approval take?

Most complete applications receive a decision within 24 hours. Applications needing document verification or business assessment take 1–3 working days. You can follow every stage in your dashboard.

What documents do I need?

National ID or passport, proof of income and proof of residence. Business applicants also provide business registration and, if available, bank or mobile money statements. Required documents are listed on each product.

Do you charge an application fee?

No. Applying and receiving a decision is free. A processing fee of 1.5–3% applies only if your loan is approved, and it is disclosed before you sign.

Can I repay early?

Yes. Early settlement quotes are available in your dashboard and from support. An early repayment charge of 0–2% of outstanding principal may apply, depending on the product.

What happens if I miss a payment?

Contact us before the due date. We will discuss a payment plan. A late charge applies after five days, and persistent non-payment may be reported to credit reference bureaus.
