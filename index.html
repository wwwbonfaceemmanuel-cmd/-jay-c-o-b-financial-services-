Why Jay C.O.B

A partner who reads the fine print — and explains it plainly.

Most financial advice in Malawi arrives with a product attached. Ours starts with your situation: your income, your dependants, your season, your risk. The recommendations follow from there.

  • Independent: we are paid for advice, not for pushing a particular bank's product.
  • Local context: plans account for kwacha volatility, seasonal cash-flow and informal income.
  • Written deliverables: you leave with a document you can act on, not a conversation you have to remember.
  • Follow-through: quarterly reviews, lender follow-ups and honest course correction.
