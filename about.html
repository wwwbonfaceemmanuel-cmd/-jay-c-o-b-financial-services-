About Jay C.O.B

A Lilongwe practice built on one belief: good advice should survive contact with real life.

Our story

Built from the paperwork nobody explained.

Our founder spent a decade inside retail banking watching the same scene repeat: capable business owners arriving with strong operations and weak files, walking out with a rejection and no explanation.

Jay C.O.B Financial Services was created to close that gap from the client's side of the desk — preparing people properly, translating the criteria, and staying in the room until a decision is made.

Today the practice works across three areas that reinforce one another: household planning, business finance, and the literacy training that makes both stick. We serve clients from Lilongwe, Blantyre, Mzuzu and Zomba, with outreach clinics in Kasungu, Salima and Mulanje.

Our promise You will never leave a meeting unsure of what happens next, what it costs, or who is responsible for it.
Mission

To make sound financial decisions ordinary — not a privilege reserved for people who already have money.

Vision

A Malawi where every household and small business has a plan it understands and can execute.

What we stand for

Four non-negotiables

Independence first

We do not accept commission-driven product quotas. Advice is the product; referrals to licensed partners are disclosed in writing.

Plain language

If a recommendation cannot be explained to a first-time borrower in one page, it is not finished.

Local reality

Seasonal income, extended-family obligations and kwacha volatility are not footnotes. They are the planning assumptions.

Measured progress

Every engagement has a baseline and a review date. If the plan is not working, we say so and change it.

The team
