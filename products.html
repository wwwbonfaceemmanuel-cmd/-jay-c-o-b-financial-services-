Packages FAQ

Fees, scope and refunds

How do fees work?

Fixed scope, fixed fee. You receive a written quote after the discovery call and pay 50% on instruction, 50% on delivery. Retainers are billed quarterly in advance.

Can fees be paid in instalments?

For household plans over MWK 150,000 we can split the fee across three months. Retainers and corporate training can be invoiced against purchase orders.

What is included in the free discovery call?

A 30-minute conversation, a preliminary view on feasibility, and a recommendation on which package fits. You do not receive a written plan at this stage.

Do you offer refunds?

If you are not satisfied with a deliverable, tell us within ten working days. We will revise it once at no cost, or refund the outstanding balance where the scope has not been performed.

Can a group share a package?

Savings circles, cooperatives and employer groups can book a shared workshop or a group Money Clarity Session at a per-participant rate. Ask for a group quote.

Is the Client Portal included?

Yes. Every paid engagement includes portal access for documents, plan milestones and statements. Portal credentials are issued once the engagement letter is signed.
