Good to know

Before you write to us

What happens after I submit the form?

A consultant reviews your enquiry, confirms the right scope, and offers two or three times for a free 30-minute call. Nothing is charged until you approve a written quote.

Is the first consultation really free?

Yes. It is a genuine fit conversation, not a sales call. You will leave with at least one actionable observation, whether or not you engage us.

Can I meet remotely?

Yes. Roughly half our clients work with us by phone, WhatsApp or video. In-person meetings are held at Area 47 or at your premises by arrangement.

How should I prepare?

Bring a recent payslip or income record, three months of bank or mobile money statements, a list of current debts, and any loan offer you have been given. If you do not have these, come anyway.

Do you handle urgent matters?

If a lender deadline is imminent, say so in your message and call the office directly. We hold limited same-week slots for time-sensitive applications.
