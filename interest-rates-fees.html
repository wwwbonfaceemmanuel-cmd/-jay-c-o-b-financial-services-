What APR means

The annual percentage rate shows the yearly cost of credit including interest and disclosed fees. It lets you compare a 24% loan with a 2% fee against a 22% loan with a 4% fee on equal terms.

Why rates differ

Unsecured lending carries more risk than secured lending, short terms need faster capital recovery, and a stronger credit record earns better pricing.

Fixed for the term

The rate in your agreement does not change during the term. Changes to our published rates apply only to new agreements.

Calculating…
Monthly and annual quotations are different units. Every rate on this page is per year (p.a.). Our calculator also accepts a rate quoted per month and applies it exactly as entered: 25% per month for one month on MWK 100,000 costs MWK 25,000 in interest, while 25% p.a. over 12 months costs the same MWK 25,000 spread over twelve instalments. Convert before comparing — 2% per month is about 24% p.a.
