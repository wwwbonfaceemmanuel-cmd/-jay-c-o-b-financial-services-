How it works

From creating your account to your final repayment, here is exactly what happens, how long it takes and what we need from you at each stage.

Create an account Check eligibility
1–10 minutes account and application 1 working day document verification 1–3 days credit assessment Same day – 48 hrs disbursement after signing

Step 1

Create your account

Register with your name, date of birth, national ID number, phone number and email address. Choose a strong password and accept the terms and privacy policy.

What you need: email or phone number, national ID, date of birth.

Timing: 2 minutes.

Create an account

Step 2

Verify your email and phone

We send a six-digit one-time code by SMS and email. Enter it to activate your account. Codes expire after 10 minutes and can be resent once every 60 seconds.

What you need: access to your phone and inbox.

Timing: under a minute.

Step 3

Choose your loan product

Compare limits, terms, rates and fees on the products page. Use the calculator to test instalments and total cost, then pick the product that fits your need and cash flow.

Tip: shorter terms cost less interest but need a larger instalment.

Compare products

Step 4

Run the numbers

Use the calculator to see the instalment, total interest, fees and full schedule. Save or print the estimate so you can compare it with your budget.

Tip: aim to keep total debt repayments below 40% of net income.

Open the calculator

Step 5

Complete the application

Five guided steps: loan details, personal and employment information, business and bank details, documents, and a final review. You get a reference number on submission and can save a draft at any point.

What you need: ID, income evidence, residence proof, bank or mobile money details.

Start an application

Step 6

Document verification

Our team checks that your ID, income evidence and residence proof are legible, current and consistent with your application. If something is missing or unclear we tell you exactly what to replace.

Timing: usually within one working day.

Step 7

Credit and affordability assessment

We review income, expenses, existing obligations and credit conduct, and may check with credit reference bureaus with your consent. Where affordability is marginal we may offer a smaller amount or a longer term.

Timing: 1–3 working days after documents are complete.

Step 8

Decision and agreement

You receive an approval, an approval with conditions, a request for more information, or a decline with reasons. If approved, the agreement shows the amount, rate, fees, schedule and total cost of credit.

Tip: read the agreement and the fee disclosure before signing.

Step 9

Disbursement

Once the agreement is signed, funds are paid to the bank or mobile money account verified in your name — usually the same day, and within 48 hours at the latest. You receive a disbursement notification and receipt.

What you need: an account in your own name and a signed agreement.

Step 10

Repay and monitor

Pay by Airtel Money, TNM Mpamba, bank transfer or standing order. Reminders arrive three days before each due date. Your dashboard shows the balance, schedule, receipts, statements and a payoff quote whenever you want it.

Tip: tell us early if your circumstances change — restructuring is always better than default.

Repayment methods. Airtel Money (merchant code JCOB), TNM Mpamba, bank transfer to our published account, or a standing order from your bank. Always use your loan reference as the payment reference, keep the confirmation SMS, and allow up to 2 hours for the payment to reflect. Report a missing payment from My transactions.

During your loan

What we do, and what we ask of you.

Our commitments

  • Reminders three days before every due date
  • Receipts for every payment, downloadable any time
  • Statements on request and monthly summaries
  • A named contact for restructuring conversations
  • Early settlement quotes within one working day
  • Complaints acknowledged within two working days

What we ask of you

  • Keep your phone number and email current
  • Never share your password or one-time codes
  • Pay on time, or call before the due date
  • Use the loan for the purpose declared
  • Tell us about changes in income or employment
  • Read statements and query anything unclear
