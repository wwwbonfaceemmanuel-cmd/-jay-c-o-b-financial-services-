    How we decide.

    Affordability

    Verified income minus essential expenses must comfortably cover the instalment. We aim for total debt repayments below 40% of net income.

    Identity & residency

    You must be 18+, resident in Malawi with a verifiable address, and able to produce valid identification.

    Credit conduct

    Existing arrears, defaults or frequent missed payments reduce affordability. A clean record or cleared arrears helps.

    Ability to document

    Complete, legible documents speed up decisions. Missing documents are the most common reason for delays.

    We may still say no. Every application is individually assessed. We decline applications we believe are unaffordable, even where the preliminary score is strong. Read our Responsible Lending Policy.
