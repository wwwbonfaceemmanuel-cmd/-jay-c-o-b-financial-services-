Flat rate explained

Interest is charged on the full principal for the whole term, even as the balance falls. It is simpler to calculate but costs more in total.

Reducing balance explained

Interest is charged on the balance you still owe each period. As the balance falls, the interest portion of each instalment falls too.

What is APR?

The annual percentage rate expresses the total cost of credit — interest plus disclosed fees — as a yearly rate, so products can be compared fairly.
