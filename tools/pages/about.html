Who we are

Overview

Local capital, local decisions, local accountability.

We were founded in Lilongwe in 2016 by a small group of credit and agribusiness professionals who were tired of watching good businesses fail for want of a predictable, fairly priced loan.

Today we lend to salaried employees, traders, farmers, cooperatives and growing companies across Malawi. Decisions are made here, by people who understand seasonal income, informal trade and the realities of running a business in a high-cost credit market.

Mission

Make responsible credit simple and accessible.

To provide transparent, affordable credit and practical financial support to Malawian households and businesses, so that borrowing builds opportunity rather than trapping people in debt.

Vision

A Malawi where good ideas can get finance.

To be the most trusted non-bank lender in Malawi — known for fair pricing, human assessment and technology that makes credit easier to understand and manage.

Transparency

Published rates, itemised fees and a total cost figure before you sign. If a charge is not disclosed, it is not payable.

Responsibility

We decline loans we believe are unaffordable and contact customers early when payments slip.

Respect

No pressure, no humiliation, no aggressive recovery. Customers in difficulty are treated with dignity.

Local roots

Malawian staff, Malawian decision-makers, and products designed around how income actually arrives here.

Our history

Ten years of steady growth.

  1. 2016 — Founded in Lilongwe

    Opened with a single office and a focus on salary-based lending for public-sector employees.

  2. 2018 — Business lending launched

    Introduced working-capital loans for registered SMEs and cooperatives, with cash-flow-based assessment.

  3. 2020 — Customer portal

    Launched online statements, repayment tracking and document uploads during the pandemic.

  4. 2022 — Agricultural finance

    Began seasonal lending with harvest-aligned repayment for farmers and agribusinesses.

  5. 2024 — Secured lending and regional reach

    Added secured facilities and expanded field operations to Blantyre, Mzuzu and Zomba.

  6. 2026 — Digital-first lending

    Rebuilt the customer experience around an online application, dashboard, and full fee transparency.

Community

Corporate social responsibility

We reinvest a share of our margin into financial literacy and enterprise support in the communities we lend to.

  • Financial literacy clinics — free monthly sessions on budgeting, saving and borrowing safely, held in Lilongwe, Blantyre and Mzuzu.
  • Women in enterprise — reduced fees and mentoring for women-led small businesses in their first two years.
  • Agribusiness partnerships — input financing and record-keeping training with farmer cooperatives.
  • School fees bridge — short-term facilities timed to school terms, with budgeting support for parents.
  • Digital access — low-bandwidth pages and USSD-style support for customers without smartphones.
