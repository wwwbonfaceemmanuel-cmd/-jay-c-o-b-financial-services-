Before you start

  • National ID or passport
  • Proof of income (three payslips or business records)
  • Proof of residence (bill or tenancy agreement)
  • Bank or mobile money details

What happens after submission

  • Confirmation by email and SMS with your reference
  • Document verification within one working day
  • Credit and affordability assessment in 1–3 working days
  • Decision, and agreement if approved

Your privacy

Your details are used only to assess this application and administer any loan. Sensitive identifiers are masked in the portal. Read the Privacy Policy, Consent & Data Retention Statement and Credit Reporting Policy.

We never ask for a fee to release a loan, or for your password or one-time code.
