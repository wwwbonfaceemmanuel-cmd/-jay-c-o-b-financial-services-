Help centre

Frequently asked questions.

How do I check the status of my application?

Sign in and open My loan applications. Each application shows its stage — pending, under review, information required, approved, rejected or withdrawn — plus reviewer comments and any outstanding documents.

What does “information required” mean?

We need something from you before we can decide — usually a clearer document, a payslip or an explanation. The application page lists exactly what is needed, with an upload or respond action.

Why was my application declined?

The most common reasons are affordability (the instalment is too high relative to income), incomplete documents, or a poor credit record. The decision message gives the reason. You can reapply after addressing it.

How long does disbursement take?

Usually the same working day after a signed agreement, and within 48 hours at the latest. Mobile money is often faster than bank transfer. You receive a disbursement notification with a receipt.

Can I change my repayment date?

In some cases. Ask support at least three working days before the due date. A change may affect the schedule and any accrued interest, and a restructuring fee may apply.

How do I get a statement?

Open Account statements in the dashboard, choose the account and period, and download or print. Statements cover loans, savings and wallet activity and carry a verification reference.

How do I close my account?

Loans must be settled first. Then request closure in Settings → Account closure. We confirm in writing, retain records required by law, and delete the rest in line with our retention policy.

How do I complain?

Use the contact form above with the category “Complaint”, email complaints@jaycobfinancial.mw, or call. You get a ticket reference, an acknowledgement within two working days and a substantive response within 15 working days.
